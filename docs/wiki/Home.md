# NextUp

> Repository copy of the published wiki [Home](https://github.com/westondunn/nextup-get-hired/wiki) page. See [README.md](README.md) in this directory.

NextUp is a self-hosted application for organising a job search. It is pre-release, runs for one person on their own machine, and is not ready for production use.

The workspace is meant to be useful on its own. Connecting a mailbox, a calendar or an AI service is optional, and none of those connections can be enabled yet.

- **What it is and its current status:** [README](../../README.md)
- **What is planned:** [Roadmap](Roadmap.md)
- **What connects, and what has been tested:** [Integrations](Integrations.md)
- **Development tooling and what has been validated:** [development notes](../DEVELOPMENT.md)
- **Branching and pull requests:** [branching guide](../BRANCHING.md)
- **Security limits and private reporting:** [security policy](../../SECURITY.md)

Outside contributions are not being accepted yet. External integrations are present in the code and switched off in every shipped configuration.
