# Prepare and synthesize representative-user validation

## Outcome

Prepare a privacy-safe protocol and synthesize human-led sessions without substituting model behavior for participant evidence.

## Baseline and authority

Stop until a live issue, clean-machine success, accessibility baseline, and maintainer-approved participant protocol exist.

## Scope

Draft the synthetic scenario, observation form, assistance rules, participant-code scheme, and evidence synthesis; analyze completed redacted records.

## Non-goals

The model does not recruit, contact, impersonate, coach, or replace participants and does not collect real job-search data.

## Interfaces and data flow

Human observers provide redacted structured notes; only approved aggregate evidence enters the repository.

## Safety and approval gates

Explicit human/maintainer approval controls participant contact, recording, retention, and publication.

## Implementation steps

Prepare protocol PR, pause for human sessions, analyze supplied redacted results, file blockers as drafts, and open evidence PR.

## Verification

Check coverage of every workflow/platform and distinguish target, completion, assistance, failure, and repeat outcome.

## Documentation and evidence

Use participant codes; record no identities, résumés, accounts, credentials, or raw sensitive notes.

## Stop conditions

Stop if consent/protocol is absent, records contain personal data, or results are too incomplete for the proposed claim.
